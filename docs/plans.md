# Plans and billing

> One flat price per machine, never per minute, and free for work in the open.

Free for public repositories and one private one, Pro at $10 a machine a month for the rest, less paid annually, never per minute.

## The two plans

| Plan | Price | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Every public repository and one private repository, on up to 2 machines. |
| Pro | $10 a machine a month, or $96 a year | Every private repository, on as many machines as you connect, on a personal account or an organisation. |

## Which plan you need

Every feature is on both plans today: teammates' laptops in the Fleet, roles from GitHub, the audit log, Trusted machines, fallback to GitHub-hosted and the CI report. Free runs your first private repository too: the first one you route. A second private repository, or a third machine, needs Pro. Until then that repository's jobs run on GitHub-hosted; no job is ever blocked.

## How billing counts

Never per minute. Pro is flat per connected machine, monthly or annually, paid from the first day. A machine you connect during a period is added at once for the rest of it, and one you remove drops off at the next renewal. You can switch between monthly and annual from the dashboard or with `buildhound billing period`; the switch applies at the next renewal and nothing is charged when you make it. Prices are before tax: VAT, GST or sales tax is added at checkout where it applies, and a business can enter its tax ID there. Organisations that join at launch keep $10 a machine a month for 12 months. Cancel any time; the plan runs to the end of the period. Your first payment is refunded on request within 30 days. The list prices of every vendor are on the [pricing page](https://buildhound.sh/pricing).
